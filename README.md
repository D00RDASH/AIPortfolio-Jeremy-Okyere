Courses:
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) ITAI 2373: This intermediate course explores the principles, methods, and applications of NLP and natural language understanding in AI and Robotics. Students will investigate how machines process, analyze, and generate human language in various forms, including text and speech. Emphasis is placed on both foundational concepts and practical skills, with hands-on experience using contemporary tools and real-world datasets. The course highlights ethical considerations and the evolving role of language technologies in AI-driven systems.

- AI Applications ITAI 2372: This is an intermediate course on the implications of AI and their impact on business and society. Students will examine specific applications of AI in business and the case histories of those implementations.  This course will cover applications ranging from healthcare and Oil & Gas to Autonomous Transportation, Manufacturing, Digital Twins, Regional Emergency Response, and Supply Chain/Logistics. The goal of this course is to teach students how to apply AI in almost any environment where people need to peacefully coexist with technology. This course focuses on the practical application of AI within real-world business, industrial, and public-sector processes. Students examine how AI systems are selected, evaluated, integrated, and governed across industries such as healthcare, manufacturing, logistics, finance, government, agriculture, and transportation. Emphasis is placed on understanding how work actually happens in organizations, identifying decision points within workflows, and determining where AI can responsibly improve efficiency, accuracy, and outcomes. Through hands-on labs, structured case analyses, process-mapping exercises, and applied projects, students develop workforce-ready skills aligned with AI analyst, AI implementation, and AI operations roles.
