This is an intermediate course on the implications of AI and its impact on business and society. Students will examine specific applications of AI in business and the case histories of those implementations.  This course will cover applications and examine how AI systems are selected, evaluated, integrated, and governed, ranging from healthcare and Oil & Gas to Autonomous Transportation, Manufacturing, Digital Twins, Regional Emergency Response, and Supply Chain/Logistics. The goal of this course is to teach students how to apply AI in almost any environment where people need to peacefully coexist with technology. Emphasis is placed on understanding how work actually happens in organizations, identifying decision points within workflows, and determining where AI can responsibly improve efficiency, accuracy, and outcomes. Through hands-on labs, structured case analyses, process-mapping exercises, and applied projects, students develop workforce-ready skills aligned with AI analyst, AI implementation, and AI operations roles.
